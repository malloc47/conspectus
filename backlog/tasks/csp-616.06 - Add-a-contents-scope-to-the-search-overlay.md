## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
`/` is where operators already go to find a session, so content search lives there as a second scope, not behind another key (operator decision 2026-10-16; menu-first discoverability). Today the overlay ranks the visible rows' metadata synchronously on every keystroke (`src/tui/widgets/search.rs`, `src/tui/search.rs`). A transcript scan can't run on the UI thread, so the contents scope drives the CSP-616.03 scanner in the background and shows results as they arrive.

Context:
- The scope (rows or contents), grouping (per session or per message), and content kinds (messages; opt-in tool and thinking content) are all visible and changeable inside the overlay, with defaults from `[search]`. Whether the contents scope spans all sessions or the active filters follows CSP-616.01.
- Typing restarts the scan after a short debounce, cancelling the previous one. The overlay shows progress (e.g. 34/77 sessions) and how many sessions were skipped.
- Per-session rows show the session label, harness badge, hit count, and best snippet. Per-message rows show one snippet per turn. Enter opens the viewer at the hit (CSP-616.05); the row-scope behavior of `/` is unchanged.
- Validate the visuals with `conspectus tui --snapshot` (which can drive overlays since TEST-008).
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
