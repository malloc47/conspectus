## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
- Why: ADR 0112 §3. Today a Related row's Preview stops at the neighbor's core fields, edge, and evidence (ADR 0107), so seeing what a link points at means drilling in.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [x] #1 With the explorer cursor on a Related row, Preview shows the `edge` and `evidence` lines as a header and the neighbor's own preview below them
- [x] #2 Unresolved-evidence rows, which have no neighbor node, keep their current preview
- [x] #3 `conspectus tui --snapshot` coverage shows a Related row to a session and one to a mux session
<!-- AC:END -->

## Implementation Plan

<!-- SECTION:PLAN:BEGIN -->
1. Resolver: the Preview's subject is the left selection, a node the explorer reaches (a Related row's neighbor, or the node drilled into), or unresolved evidence. `node_source` maps a node to its source: a session to its transcript (its pane when `session_preview = pane` and it is in tmux), a mux session to its pane, other kinds nothing yet. `RowPreview::Link` exposes the neighbor id.
2. Renderer: a Related row keeps its neighbor, edge, and evidence lines as a header; the neighbor's preview renders below, bottom-anchored for a transcript. A neighbor with no source shows its core fields, as today. Unresolved rows are unchanged. A drilled-into node's field rows show that node's preview instead of the left selection's.
3. Loading and scrolling follow the resolved source, so a neighbor's transcript or pane loads when the cursor lands on its row.
4. Tests: resolver and renderer unit tests, and `tui --snapshot` coverage of a Related row to a session and one to a mux session.
<!-- SECTION:PLAN:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
- Field rows of a drilled-into node now preview that node; before, they showed the left selection's preview.
- A mux session hosting this TUI has no pane source from either path, so a Related row to it shows its fields.
- Snapshot mode still doesn't capture panes, so the mux case in the snapshot test asserts the evidence header over the pane's "loading" placeholder.
<!-- SECTION:NOTES:END -->

## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
A Related row's Preview keeps its neighbor, `edge`, and `evidence` lines as a header and shows the neighbor's own preview below (a session's transcript, a mux session's pane); neighbors without a preview yet keep their core fields, and unresolved rows are unchanged. The resolver now picks a subject (selection, explorer node, or unresolved) before a source, and `node_source` is the per-kind hook the remaining subtasks extend. Verified with `just check` (2,248 tests), including `tui --snapshot` tests for a Related row to a session and to a mux session, and a live snapshot.
