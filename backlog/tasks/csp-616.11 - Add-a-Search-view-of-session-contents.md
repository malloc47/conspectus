## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A dedicated place to see search results: a sixth view tab that lists sessions matching the active content query, ranked, with hit counts and snippets, and per-message hits when grouped that way. Enter opens the viewer at the hit. It follows the wiring pattern of the Pinned and History views and the column principles in docs/tui-columns.md.
